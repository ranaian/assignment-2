Campus Food Blog

The purpose of this blog is to recommend restaurants within walking distance of UNCG. Life can be hectic, and getting food shouldn't be something you dread. With this short list, you can find something you like to eat without having to run across town for your next meal. Try out one of these restaurants next time you're looking for a meal!

Old Town Draught House is a tavern on Spring Garden St that serves sandwiches and burgers, as well as alcoholic beverages for of-age patrons.
Old Town Draught House is open 7 days a week 11 am to 11 pm
The food has a premium price but is high quality, good for a fine lunch or dinner out with friends without the need to stray far from campus. My favorite is the Old Town Burger with house-fried chips.
It appears that the Old Town Burger has been removed from the menu, according to the website. Another favorite of mine is the Black and Blue, a blackened burger with bacon, blue cheese, mushrooms, red onions, and a barbeque aioli.

Slices Pizza by Tony is an Italian restaurant on Tate St the specializes in pizza but serves other popular Italian dishes.
Slices is open Monday through Saturday, 11am to 11pm
I'm fond of their chicken stromboli, and for dessert I like a zeppole.

Chipotle is a chain restaurant on Tate St. that serves burritos.
Chipotle is open 7 days a week 10:45 am to 11 pm
I typically order a chicken burrito with brown rice, black beans, sour cream, pico de gallo, shredded cheese, and lettuce.
